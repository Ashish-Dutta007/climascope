# Dynamic wall scenes, 13 October 2026

The wall now offers several coordinated views of the same selected month, region and ensemble. A new wall defaults to a focused water-balance scene. A saved scene is restored on reload, and the nine-map overview remains available. All map colours remain absolute monthly climatologies on fixed annual scales.

## Using the views

- `/immersive`: suite wall. Pair a phone using **Phone remote**.
- `/immersive/control`: scene selection, metric selection for ensemble agreement, month, member, council/catchment and playback.
- `/immersive/preview`: live wall renderer inside a fitted desktop page. Select open or full-wrap dimensions, a scene and a preview scale. Use 25%, 50% or 100% to scroll through a section. Copy this view produces a link preserving scene, focus metric, month, member, region and wall size.

The preview is independent of the suite wall. It uses an isolated channel and a parent/iframe message token, does not initialize the phone relay, and does not read or write the ordinary wall's saved state. Only a preview wall can be embedded, and only by the same origin; the main viewer, normal wall and controller retain their deny-framing headers.

| Compare all metrics | Nine maps, all three periods for all three metrics | Seasonal values and monthly change, per metric |
| Water balance | Three water-balance maps; later-period precipitation and temperature maps beside them | Seasonal absolute values and paired monthly change bars |

Focused scenes show five maps and two charts. The supporting areas retain all three period means and label their displayed later-period map. The chart column stays at 820 wall pixels in both room configurations. Overview groups are capped at 3,600 pixels, and focus groups at 6,200 pixels; extra full-wrap width separates groups rather than stretching charts. Context is repeated within each group. The insight card supplies a question, a labelled headline statistic and the relevant interpretation.

## Playback

**Annual cycle** advances through all twelve months in the current scene. **Guided tour** follows eight chapters for the selected area and member: January overview; July temperature; July precipitation; July water balance; negative-balance extent; land-cover context; water-balance ensemble agreement; January water-balance contrast. The tour then repeats.

At the default six-second monthly setting, simple chapters hold for twelve seconds and the more involved chapters for eighteen seconds, after render readiness. The tour takes roughly two minutes plus any loading time. The dwell setting also controls the tour, with a ten-second minimum per chapter. Manual selection pauses playback. Play resumes the selected mode; Annual cycle explicitly returns to monthly playback; Guided tour starts at chapter one.

The wall owns both timers. Closing the controller does not stop playback. Scene, focus metric, playback mode, tour chapter, month, member and region persist on the wall. Controller reloads mirror them without resetting the display.

## New data and its meaning

`tools/precompute_immersive_scenes.py` writes `static/immersive/summaries/scenes-v1/`. Run it from the app's venv after the existing v2 regional summaries are current. It checks the fingerprint of all 972 source climate partitions against v2, records a separate land-cover data fingerprint, and writes thirteen CWB summary files plus three metric-specific member-change files.

**Cell shares:** the numerator is the number of valid common-support climate cells with absolute CWB strictly below zero; the denominator is the number of common-support climate cells intersecting the region. The generator verifies that each member has identical support before reusing the denominator. The observed baseline comes from `obs_value`; futures come from `proj_value`. The statistic is not inferred from regional means.

National July checks on 78,855 valid climate cells:

| Period | Cells with CWB below zero |
|---|---:|
| Observed baseline 1990–2019 | 57.4% |
| 2020–2049 | 68.9% |
| 2050–2079 | 81.4% |

These are cell shares, not precisely surveyed or region-clipped land areas. Negative monthly climatological balance does not establish hydrological drought or an observed impact.

**Land-cover overlap:** for each class, sum `lc.frac` in valid cells below the threshold and divide by `lc.frac` across the same valid support. Existing land cover is held fixed across the periods. The chart compares baseline and 2050–2079 within each class; the five classes are chosen by represented fraction weight, not by maximum threshold share. It uses whole intersecting cells and does not claim precise clipped catchment area or future habitat loss. This calculation deliberately does not reuse `/api/coverage`, whose existing thresholds concern the `Change` column.

**Ensemble agreement:** each member's regional mean is calculated first in v2, then differenced from the observed regional baseline. The scene shows all available individual changes and their full range/median. It does not average cellwise percentiles into a regional interval or label ensemble spread as probability. Maps and ordinary means continue to follow the selected member; the range charts explicitly cover all twelve available members.

New artifacts are loaded only by scenes that need them. The renderer retains the mean and one selected-member summary, plus one metric-specific agreement file. Staged frames retain their exact summary references, so a late response from an earlier member cannot change the displayed figures. Climate requests remain bounded to one in flight plus one next-frame cache; the tour prefetches its next month instead of an unused monthly sequence.

## Verification and release

The precomputation reconciles July national cell counts and fraction-weighted land-cover numerators against independent DuckDB queries for all twelve members and the mean. Artifact provenance and checks are in `scenes-v1/validation.json`.

All 17 automated tests passed on the local four-worker staging server: thirteen relay tests, two existing wall/phone flows, and two new scene/preview flows. The complete tour retained 195.9 MB before and 184.0 MB after garbage collection. No uncaught browser errors were recorded.

Real Chromium checks cover all seven scenes at both documented wall sizes, phone selection, member-specific Midlothian data, phone/wall reload, a complete guided tour with the controller page closed, bounded memory, isolated preview state, a failed scene request preserving the previous frame, a late scene response losing to the latest selection, reproducible preview links and a catchment with no climate data. The earlier annual-playback/phone flows remain in the regression suite. See [scene validation](scenes-validation-2026-10-13/).

Room evaluation still needs the mapping from browser pixels to physical viewing sectors, actual projection contrast, safe-band calibration, and readability from seated, standing and rear positions. The focus layout is arranged on the logical canvas; the room's front-facing sector may require a different placement. The PT method expansion and authoritative scenario wording remain subject to scientific confirmation.

Further scene additions should answer a new question. Habitat and soil-wetness overlap, explicitly labelled change maps, and linked selected-place landscape views are candidates. They should use checked quantities and denominators, with scene-specific legends and reproducible state, rather than loading every layer onto the overview.
