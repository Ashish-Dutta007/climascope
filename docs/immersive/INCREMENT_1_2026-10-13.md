## What changed

**Layout** (`static/immersive/immersive.js`, `immersive.css`, `templates/immersive.html`)

- Each metric group is a CSS grid: three equal map columns plus one chart column. The metric title is centred over the three maps. Each regional mean sits under its own map, with its unit. Future columns add a signed "Δ … from baseline".
- One shared legend per group sits in the middle map's empty surround. It is placed by testing candidate corners against the region outline (or Scotland's councils), and has an opaque backing. The locator inset uses the same placement in the baseline map.
- Every group repeats the month, region and ensemble, plus month progress and playback mode. The global selection strip is gone.
- The chart column holds two stacked charts:
  - all three periods of monthly climatology;
  - monthly change from the 1990–2019 baseline for both futures, with a zero line.

  Period colours and dash styles are shared across the wall. Gaps stay as gaps, and the axes do not move with the month.
- Absolute values no longer carry a leading plus. Changes are always signed.
- Grid cells without climate values use a distinct no-data colour, and the legend includes it.

**Colour domains.** Each metric has a fixed domain for the whole annual loop, from 0.5–99.5% quantiles of ensemble-mean cells across all months and periods. Arrow caps mark values beyond the domain. Domains: CWB PT −70 to 340 mm (white at 0), precipitation 40 to 350 mm, Tmax 2 to 24 °C.

**Regional summaries.** `static/immersive/summaries/v2/` replaces `region_monthly.json`.

- `mean.json` holds the ensemble mean for all three periods, cell counts, domains and provenance.
- `member_<id>.json` holds the future periods for each member. The baseline is observed and shared; the generator asserts this.
- Values are stored to 3 dp, so displayed changes are differenced before rounding.
- Means use the common support across periods. Every metric and month has 78,855 valid cells nationally, with no exceptions.
- Regenerate with `venv/bin/python tools/precompute_immersive_summaries.py` (about 2 minutes). It reconciles national July values against the review and every national mean against DuckDB.

**Mean/member consistency.** Maps, numbers and charts all follow the selected member.

- `/api/immersive_values` now returns `_meta`, which records whether each metric and period is observed, mean or member, and lists any member→mean substitution.
- The wall labels a substituted map "ensemble mean (member NN unavailable)" and uses the mean for its numbers too.

**Payload.**

- `?metrics=` restricts the payload to the drawn panels.
- Map values are rounded to 0.01. One month is now about 3.5 MB brotli, down from 7.8 MB.

**Playback and control** (`control.js`, `templates/immersive_control.html`)

- The wall is the single playback authority. The controller sends commands (`set`, `step`, `play`, `pause`, `dwell`) and mirrors the wall's reports, so a hidden or closed controller cannot drive or reset playback. The wall also sends a heartbeat every 10 s.
- Each frame is staged:
  1. Map data, summaries and region geometry load first.
  2. Labels, numbers, charts and maps commit together.
  3. The frame counts as ready only after all nine maps report `idle`.
  4. Dwell starts from that point.
- On failure, the previous frame stays up with its own labels, and playback pauses with a visible message.
- Any manual selection pauses playback. Dec → Jan shows "↺ New annual cycle".
- At most one payload request is in flight at a time, plus a single prefetched next frame. Superseded requests are allowed to finish rather than aborted, and only the latest target is requested next.
- State (month, member, region, playing, dwell, revision) persists in `localStorage`, so a wall reload resumes where it was.
- Tiny catchments are widened to a 0.02° frame instead of being rejected. Previously 79 catchments silently fell back to All Scotland.

## Kiosk URL options

| Option | Effect | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `?safe=<px>` | Bottom band kept free of essential text; only the footer sits in it | 56 |
| `?dwell=<s>` | Seconds per month during playback (2–60; adjustable from the controller) | 6 |
| `?autoplay=1` | Start playing on load | restore saved state |
| `?fresh=1` | Ignore saved state | — |

Sizes are authored for a 1200 px tall canvas and scale down proportionally in smaller preview windows.

## Verification (headless Chromium, 13 October 2026)

- Rendered at 11,514 × 1,200 and 15,360 × 1,200. Islands, legends and charts were checked visually. Every cell with climate data lies inside the wall's Scotland bounds.
- An end-to-end controller flow passed 32 of 32 checks at open mode. The same flow ran at full wrap, where the only failing check was later traced to the test and corrected. The flow covered:
  - council, member and catchment selection;
  - playback across Dec → Jan, with ready-to-ready gaps of at least the dwell;
  - manual pause;
  - rapid clicks, with never more than one request in flight;
  - reload restore;
  - an injected HTTP 500, after which the old frame was kept and playback paused;
  - a catchment with no climate cells.
- Memory: retained JS heap after GC stayed flat at 160–177 MB over 26 frames (two annual resets). The first frame was ready in about 4–6 s with software WebGL.
- National July numbers match the review: CWB 3.2 → −24.9 mm (Δ −28.1), precipitation 94.5 → 82.3 mm (Δ −12.2), Tmax 16.4 → 21.1 °C (Δ +4.8).
