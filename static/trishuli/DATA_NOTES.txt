Purpose
-------
This package supports an open source-to-impact reconstruction and subsequent
imagery or field review of the Bhote Koshi-Trishuli flood. It is not a flood
extent, damage assessment, hazard model, evacuation map or official emergency
product.

Event evidence
--------------
event_evidence.json contains the reviewed USGS landslide time and location,
Copernicus DEM endpoint measurements, attributed event observations and a
snapshot of five Nepal DHM gauge series retrieved for 26 August 2026.

event_observations.geojson contains the USGS source point and five DHM
station points. Evidence-tier and source URL fields are retained. The source
point is not a collapse polygon. Four returned gauge series end between 08:40
and 09:20 NPT; Dhunche continues beyond the event window. A series ending is
not a flood-wave arrival time.

The USGS catalogue record is mutable. Event us7000tbwb was first returned as
an mb 4.4 earthquake at 02:52:47 UTC and was subsequently revised to a reviewed
M 5.2 Landslide at 02:52:10 UTC. Version 0.4.1 uses the later reviewed product.
USGS key points state that nearby seismic stations, long-period waves and
satellite imagery led to its determination of a glacial collapse and debris
flow, and that no earthquake occurred. USGS says the source location was
estimated from satellite images. That establishes the source classification,
not the complete downstream flood sequence or water balance.

districts.geojson contains the Rasuwa and Nuwakot ADM2 polygons from the Nepal
Common Operational Dataset for Administrative Boundaries (COD-AB). Source:
Survey Department of Nepal and UN Resident Coordinator's Office. The source
boundary is valid from 14 March 2024 and distributed under CC BY-IGO.

Pre-event package
-----------------
trishuli_corridor.gpkg contains ten EPSG:4326 layers:

  main_stem       mapped river centreline
  channel_buffers 100 m, 250 m and 500 m centreline buffers
  reach_metrics   1 km terrain samples from Copernicus DEM GLO-30
  buildings       OSM building footprints with centroid distance to channel
  roads           OSM road lines with distance to channel
  bridges         OSM bridge features with centroid distance to channel
  education       OSM education features with centroid distance to channel
  health          OSM health features with centroid distance to channel
  aeroway         OSM aeroway and helipad features with distance to channel
  places          OSM populated-place features with distance to channel

Rapid post-event analyses
--------------------------
The 12 and 27 August Sentinel-2 comparison uses one 45RUM grid, one radiometric
stretch, and only pixels clear on both dates. Within 600 m of the mapped
channel, MNDWI > 0 area changes from 0.932 to 1.721 square kilometres. Across
46 fully clear transects, median wetted width changes from 60 to 120 m and 40
transects are wider. These are date-to-date observations, not a flood extent or
causal attribution.

Open-Meteo hourly fields were sampled at 14 source-catchment and corridor
points. Four high-altitude source samples total 0.5-0.8 mm in the 72 hours
before onset and 0.1-0.3 mm in the final 24 hours. This is a coarse model-grid
screen, not a rain-gauge result, and may miss local orographic precipitation.

The Planet Crisis Response collection on source.coop was visually reviewed.
The post-event SkySat acquisitions are approximately 0.8 m, but opaque cloud at
Rasuwagadhi and valley haze at Syabrubesi obscure the target channel. The
PlanetScope pre/post assets also mix surface-reflectance and top-of-atmosphere
products. No quantitative Planet change result is produced.

Analytical limits
-----------------
- OSM completeness is uneven. Counts describe mapped features only.
- Building and polygon distances use centroids, not nearest polygon edges.
- The mapped centreline and its buffers are screening geometries.
- The BSI field is an experimental, uncalibrated composite retained from the
  rapid build. Do not interpret it as blockage probability or hazard. Prefer
  the direct slope, relief and gradient fields.
- The public figure includes a post-event Sentinel-2 derivative; no original
  post-event image product or field validation is included in the downloads.
- Four DHM gauge series end during the event window and do not capture the
  peak; Dhunche continues and is shown as such.
- Proximity to the channel does not imply that a feature was flooded,
  damaged or inaccessible.
