What the evidence shows

Bhote Koshi–Trishuli flood, 26 August 2026

Ashish Dutta · Geospatial researcher

Not emergency guidance. This is a working reconstruction from public spatial evidence, not an official flood or damage assessment.

I have been piecing together the public evidence from the 26 August flood. Three signals stand out: a wider downstream channel in the first usable image, a revised USGS source record, and river gauges that stop reporting during the event.

Finding 1

Only channel sections clear on both dates were compared. This is a measured image difference, not a flood outline or a claim that every change was caused by the event.

Finding 2

USGS revised the source record

The event was first listed as an earthquake. USGS now says the long-period seismic signal came from a glacial collapse and debris flow, and that no earthquake occurred. Its source point was estimated from satellite imagery.

On the map, that point is 13.5 km in a straight line from Rasuwagadhi and 3,838 m higher. It is a useful source reference, not a mapped collapse scar or flow path.

Finding 3

Four returned gauge series go quiet

A final dashboard reading below warning level is only the last sample received. It is not the flood peak. A series ending may reflect an instrument, power or communications failure; I do not use it as a flood-arrival time.

  • HOT Tasking Manager and OpenStreetMap contributors: live humanitarian mapping context under ODbL.

The optical measurement uses a common image stretch and pixels clear on both dates within 600 m of the mapped channel. Threshold sensitivity changes the magnitude, but not the direction, of the measured difference.

A roughly 20 million m³ supraglacial-lake explanation circulating for Rasuwa refers to the July 2025 flood, not this event.

What is not claimed
  • No flood extent, depth, discharge, affected-asset count or casualty estimate is produced.
  • Gauge silence is not proof of instrument destruction.
  • The preliminary blockage account is attributed, not independently verified here.
