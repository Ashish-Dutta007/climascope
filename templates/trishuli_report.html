01

What we know now

The language below follows the strength of the evidence, not the urgency of the story.

Confirmed in retrieved sources

A large flood moved through the river system

ICIMOD reports rapid water-level rise from about 09:00 NPT, with water, sediment and boulders moving from the Bhote Koshi into the Trishuli.

The current USGS catalogue classifies the 02:52:10 UTC seismic source as an M 5.2 landslide, not an earthquake.

Best current interpretation

An ice-rock avalanche is suspected

ICIMOD and partners are investigating whether an ice-rock avalanche entered the Lhende Khola and generated or released the surge.

A temporary channel blockage may be part of the sequence. The source mechanism and blockage history remain unconfirmed.

Not known from this analysis

The footprint and affected assets

There is no satellite-derived flood or debris extent here, no measured discharge series, and no verified count of affected buildings, roads or bridges.

Proximity to the pre-event channel is exposure, never proof of damage.

The corridor before the flood

A consistent baseline for orienting imagery review and field reports, not an impact map.

OpenStreetMap features from the HOT/HDX extract dated 24 August 2026. Rural mapping is incomplete, and distances use the mapped channel centreline.

Read this as

4,619 mapped building footprints have centroids within 250 m of the channel. This is useful for prioritising later image interpretation.

It does not mean 4,619 buildings flooded. Only an observed extent or verified field report can support that claim.

Terrain shapes the problem

The upper gorge is steep and confined; the valley opens progressively toward Bidur.

04

Rainfall is context, not a verdict

07

Sources and boundaries

Enough detail to audit the brief without making the main story carry the whole notebook.

Primary sources
  • USGS event record — event type, magnitude, location and origin time.
  • ICIMOD media advisory — observed river behaviour and current scientific interpretation.
  • HOT/HDX Nepal flood extract — OpenStreetMap snapshot dated 24 August 2026, ODbL.
  • Copernicus Data Space and Copernicus DEM GLO-30 — acquisition catalogue and terrain.
  • Open-Meteo forecast API — archived model forecasts and future outlook; no gauge observations.
Analytical boundaries
  • The mapped baseline runs from Rasuwagadhi to the HOT extraction limit below Bidur. It does not cover the full downstream Trishuli and Narayani system.
  • Building distances use footprint centroids. OSM completeness varies, so totals are an inventory of mapped features rather than the complete exposed population.
  • Terrain derivatives use a 30 m surface model. No terrain score is presented as calibrated hazard.
  • No casualty figure, affected-asset count, flood depth or causal attribution is produced by this work.
Download and reuse

The GeoPackage contains ten baseline layers for QGIS or ArcGIS Pro. Read the accompanying data notes before reuse. OSM-derived content is available under ODbL; retain source attribution.
